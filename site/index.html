The brain01

A real wiring diagram,simulated cell by cell.

C. elegans was the first animal whose whole nervous system was mapped, one neuron at a time. SPOOR builds its brain straight from that map: 302 neurons, each a leaky integrate-and-fire cell, connected only where the map says they connect.

Nothing is tuned in secret. Where a synapse sign or a constant had to be calibrated, the lab names it.

How it trades02

In nature, this brain climbs a scent gradient toward food. SPOOR does the same with markets: buying pressure is the scent, and the brain crawls toward wherever the smell is strongest.

It hunts marketsthe way it hunts food.
