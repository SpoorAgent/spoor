How it works:
1. Three scout brains turn onchain flow, 15-min price moves and news into smell
2. The smell hits the sensory neurons
3. Spikes travel only along real synapses
4. Forward neurons win twice in a row: it strikes. Reverse neurons win: it turns away

Paper trading only. No wallet. No language model making calls.
